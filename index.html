Install Member Information Hub On Your Phone

You can save this page directly to your home screen for quick, one-tap access:

Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet):

  1. Tap the menu icon (three dots in top-right).
  2. Select Install and create shortcut (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm by tapping Install or Add.

iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Privacy/Data Protection

1.
This online NCH Cór Linn Information Hub (“the App”) is a spinoff of somewhat similar hub that was developed, on his own initiative, by Kevin O’Connell, a member of the National Symphony Chorus. To contact him please click here.
2.
An Ceoláras Náisiúnta (the National Concert Hall) has not been involved in the development of the App. Neither is it involved in any control, maintenance or monitoring of it.
3.
The App is not intended to be used for gathering or disseminating anyone's personal data. No such data is collected when Cór Linn Members, or their parents/guardians, choose whether or not to use the App. Similarly, no logs are maintained regarding anyone’s use of it.
4.
The Cór Linn Music Director and Choir Manager have both undertaken that no personal data will ever be uploaded by them to the “Current Notes” section of the App.
5.
Cór Linn Members and their parents remain perfectly free to choose not to download or use the App. Information regarding rehearsals & performances will continue to be communicated to them in exactly the same way as prior to the development of the App.

Child Protection & Safeguarding

Cór Linn and the National Concert Hall are committed to the highest standards of child protection and safeguarding. All staff, conductors, and volunteers adhere to strict safeguarding policies to ensure a safe, inclusive, and nurturing environment for all young choir members.

Further details regarding our safeguarding guidelines, designated liaison persons, and reporting procedures will be made available here.
